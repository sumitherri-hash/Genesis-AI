Neural Lab

Three optional model families for pattern learning. They are tools for specific tasks—not a replacement for GENESIS's language model, evidence checks, or human review.

Autoencoders

Compress numeric inputs into a smaller representation and reconstruct them. Reconstruction error can help flag unusual business or quality metrics.

Requires representative training data and validation.

Recurrent Neural Networks

Process ordered sequences. Potential uses include time-series trends, demand forecasting, and sequence classification.

Compare against simple forecasting baselines before trusting predictions.

Variational Autoencoders

Learn a probabilistic latent space and generate synthetic numeric samples for controlled experiments.

Synthetic output is never real-world evidence.
